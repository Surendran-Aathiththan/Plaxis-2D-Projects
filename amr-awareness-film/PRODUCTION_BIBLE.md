# Production bible: characters, look, voices, music and sound

Every shot prompt in [SHOT_LIST.md](SHOT_LIST.md) refers to the **locked descriptions** below. Paste the relevant block word for word into every generation prompt (and upload the same reference image every time) so faces, clothes and hair never drift between shots.

## Global style lock

> **STYLE:** High-end stylised 3D animated feature film look, soft subsurface skin shading, expressive large eyes, smooth cinematic camera, natural global illumination with warm tropical sunlight, shallow depth of field, rich but natural colour grade, 16:9, 1920×1080, 25 fps. Sri Lankan setting and people. No text on screen unless specified.

## Characters (locked descriptions)

**STUDENT 1 (the patient)**
> 16-year-old Sri Lankan Tamil schoolboy, slim build, medium-brown skin, short neat black hair parted on the left, thin face, dark brown eyes. All-white Sri Lankan school uniform: short-sleeved white shirt with a school crest on the left pocket, white trousers, maroon-and-gold striped tie, black shoes, navy-blue backpack. Rides a green bicycle. Scene 1: tired eyes, hand on throat. Scene 5 onward: pale, sweaty, dark circles, in a light-blue hospital gown.

**STUDENT 2 (the friend)**
> 16-year-old Sri Lankan Tamil schoolboy, slightly stocky, medium-dark skin, thick curly black hair a little messy, big toothy grin, round cheeks, lively eyebrows. Same all-white uniform and maroon-and-gold tie, worn loosely with the top button open, sleeves slightly rolled, red backpack. Rides a red bicycle with a bell. Always energetic and animated.

**PHARMACIST**
> Sri Lankan man about 40, neat moustache, rectangular glasses, short side-parted hair, white pharmacist coat over a light-blue shirt, small name badge. Friendly and casual. Works behind a glass counter in a small, cluttered community pharmacy.

**MOTHER**
> Sri Lankan Tamil woman about 45, warm brown skin, hair in a low bun, small red pottu on her forehead, simple maroon cotton saree with a thin gold border, thin gold chain, cloth handbag. Worried face, hands clasped.

**CONSULTANT PHYSICIAN**
> Sri Lankan man about 52, salt-and-pepper short hair, rimless glasses, clean-shaven, calm kind eyes. Long white coat over a light-blue shirt and dark tie, stethoscope around his neck, hospital ID badge clipped to the coat pocket. Calm, compassionate, authoritative posture.

**CONSULTANT MICROBIOLOGIST**
> Sri Lankan man about 40, short black hair, neatly trimmed short beard, square black-framed glasses. White lab coat over a grey shirt, hospital ID badge on a blue lanyard, often holding a printed laboratory report or a tablet. Clear, confident, educational manner.

**BACTERIA AND MICROBES** (all recognisably bacteria: rod- or round-shaped cells with a visible cell wall, pili and flagella; faces are simple painted-on expressions)

| Group | Look | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| Sensitive bacteria | Small, round or short-rod cells in **sky blue and mint green**, big friendly eyes | Burst open (cell wall cracks, then lysis) when amoxicillin reaches them |
| Beneficial bacteria (normal flora) | Soft **teal and leaf-green** rods with gentle smiles and a tiny heart-shaped glow | Also knocked down and wilting when the antibiotic arrives (collateral damage) |
| Resistant bacteria | Larger rods, **crimson red and deep purple**, thick glossy capsule, spiky pili, eyebrows, mischievous grins | Survive the antibiotic, then multiply by **splitting in two** (binary fission) |
| **The Boss** (speaking resistant bacterium) | Biggest purple-red rod, gold-tinted flagellum like a tail, one raised eyebrow, comic-villain grin | Leads the gang, talks to the camera |
| Amoxicillin | Small glowing **yellow-white molecules with a four-sided (beta-lactam) ring** | Drift in through the blood. Against resistant cells, tiny enzyme "scissors" (beta-lactamase) snip the ring and it fades |
| Virus (Scene 6 only) | Small spiky grey-violet sphere | Antibiotic molecules bounce off it harmlessly |
| Blood cells | Red biconcave discs, a few white blood cells | Flow past in the vessel |

**Science lock for every microscopic shot:** the resistant bacteria are already present, in small numbers, among the sensitive ones *before* the tablet is taken. The antibiotic does not make them decide to resist. It kills the sensitive ones, leaving room for the resistant few to multiply. Never show a bacterium "seeing" the drug and transforming.

## Voice casting

Use real Sri Lankan Tamil voice actors (a school or university drama society works well), recorded in a quiet room at 48 kHz / 24-bit. Use a separate, consistent voice for every role and **keep the same actor for every line of a role**.

| Role | Voice | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Student 1 | Young male, mid-teens | Scene 1: hoarse, tired, slightly nasal. Scene 3: relieved, casual |
| Student 2 | Young male, mid-teens | Bright, cheeky, quick. Sings the Scene 2 song off-key on purpose |
| Pharmacist | Adult male | No scripted line. Record a friendly "ம்ம்… இந்தாங்க" (*mm… here you go*) wild line only if the edit needs it |
| Resistant bacterium (Boss) | Male, character voice | Comic villain: sly, sing-song, theatrical laugh. In the close-up, drop to a slow, low whisper |
| Mother | Female, about 45 | Anxious, breathless, close to tears |
| Consultant physician | Mature male | Calm, warm, unhurried, authoritative |
| Consultant microbiologist | Male, about 40 | Crisp, clear, teacher-like |
| Narrator | Deep male | Serious, measured, slight reverb |

Pronounce the English medical words (Amoxicillin, antibiotic, culture report, susceptibility, investigations, Antimicrobial Resistance) the way Sri Lankan Tamil speakers naturally say them in conversation, not in an exaggerated accent.

Tamil text-to-speech is not a substitute here: the brief rules out robotic voices, and the comedy in Scenes 1–4 depends on timing and attitude.
