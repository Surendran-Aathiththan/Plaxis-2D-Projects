# Shot list and generation prompts

Total running time is **180 s** in seven scene clips. Times are film timecode. A text in brackets, such as **[STYLE]** or **[STUDENT 1]**, means *paste that locked block from [PRODUCTION_BIBLE.md](PRODUCTION_BIBLE.md) here*.

How to use this with a video generator:

1. Generate each **shot** (most tools make 5–10 s per generation) silently or with ambience only. Ask for natural mouth movement on talking shots. Real Tamil voices are added afterwards and lip-synced (see [README.md](README.md)).
2. Use the same character reference images, seed and style lock for every shot.
3. Cut the shots of a scene together to **exactly** the scene length and export them as `clips/sceneN.mp4`.
4. Dialogue slots match `subtitles/amr_film_en.srt` exactly. Record to these slots and the subtitles stay in sync.
